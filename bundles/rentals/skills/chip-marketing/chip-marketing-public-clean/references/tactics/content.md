# Content & Growth Tactics

## 5. Content Syndication Network

Write one blog post. an agent runtime transforms it into:
- Medium article (reformatted)
- Dev.to post (with tags/series)
- Hashnode article (with canonical URL)
- LinkedIn article (professional angle)
- X/Twitter thread (key points extracted)
- Reddit posts to 5 relevant subreddits (different angles each)

Each version is platform-native, not copy-paste. Then it monitors engagement and reports which platforms drive the most traffic.

**Skills:** Content transformation, multi-platform posting, analytics tracking

## 16. Support-to-Content Pipeline

Connect an agent runtime to your support inbox.

It analyzes common questions and frustrations. Identifies patterns. Generates content briefs for help articles, blog posts, and product improvements.

Example: "40% of tickets this month mention confusion about X feature. Here's a tutorial draft that addresses the top 5 misunderstandings."

Your support tickets become your content calendar.

**Skills:** Support analysis, pattern detection, content generation

## 17. Affiliate Army Builder

an agent runtime identifies potential affiliates by:
- Finding bloggers who rank for your target keywords but don't mention your product
- Analyzing their existing affiliate relationships
- Generating personalized affiliate recruitment pitches
- Managing application and onboarding flow
- Tracking affiliate performance

Turn content creators into your sales force, automatically.

**Skills:** Affiliate research, outreach automation, relationship management

## 18. PR Newsjacking Bot

Monitor breaking news in your industry. When something relevant happens, an agent runtime:
- Drafts rapid response or commentary
- Identifies journalists who covered similar stories
- Generates personalized pitches positioning you as a source
- Sends outreach within hours of the news breaking

Journalists need expert sources. You become the expert who responds fastest.

**Skills:** News monitoring, journalist research, rapid outreach

## 19. A/B Test Automator

an agent runtime manages your landing page testing:
- Generates headline variations
- Implements tests through your landing page builder API
- Monitors statistical significance
- Automatically promotes winners
- Generates new variations to test against the winner

Continuous optimization without manual intervention.

**Skills:** Copy generation, API integration, statistical analysis

## 20. Full-Funnel Attribution Detective

**Prompt:**
> "Trace the journey of every customer who signed up this month. What was their first touchpoint? What content did they consume? What finally converted them?"

an agent runtime pulls data from your analytics, CRM, and ad platforms. Correlates signals. Builds journey maps for your actual customers.

Example output: "Your best best customers read an average of 3.2 blog posts before converting, with the 'comparison guide' being the most common final touchpoint before signup."

**Skills:** Data correlation, attribution modeling, insight generation
