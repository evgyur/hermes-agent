# Monitoring Tactics

## 3. Competitor Shadow

**Prompt:**
> "Monitor [competitor.com] for any changes. New features, pricing updates, blog posts, job listings, social media announcements. Summarize what changed and alert me within 15 minutes."

an agent runtime checks their site, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, changelog, job board. Correlates signals.

Example: New engineering hires + ML job post + blog silence = they're building something.

**Skills:** Web monitoring, multi-source aggregation, pattern detection

## 4. Review Farm Defense System

Set up alerts for new reviews across G2, Capterra, TrustPilot, Product Hunt, X/Twitter.

**Positive review:**
- Automatic thank-you response within 2 hours
- Extract best quote for social proof
- Add reviewer to champions list
- Generate personalized case study request

**Negative review:**
- Instant alert to your phone
- Draft empathetic response for approval
- Log complaint in feedback tracker
- Tag relevant team members

**Skills:** Multi-platform monitoring, sentiment analysis, templated responses

## 8. SEO Gap Assassin

**Prompt:**
> "Analyze my top 10 competitors. Find keywords they rank for that I don't. Prioritize by search volume and difficulty. Generate content briefs for the top 20 gaps."

an agent runtime pulls SERP data, cross-references with your existing content, identifies gaps, and generates actionable briefs.

Optional: It can draft the actual content or hand it to Claude Code to build interactive tools targeting those keywords.

**Skills:** SEO analysis, competitor research, content brief generation

## 9. Social Proof Collector

**Prompt:**
> "Find every mention of [brand] on X/Twitter, Reddit, LinkedIn, Hacker News from the last 30 days. Extract positive quotes. Format them with author name, title, and link for testimonials."

an agent runtime builds your social proof library automatically. It suggests which quotes work best for which pages (homepage vs. pricing vs. specific feature pages).

**Skills:** Social listening, quote extraction, organization

## 10. Hacker News Timing Bot

HN timing matters. Post at the wrong hour and you're buried.

an agent runtime monitors HN activity patterns for your topic area. When it detects optimal conditions (active relevant users, low competition, favorable moderation), it alerts you.

Advanced: The agent drafts multiple HN-style titles, tests them for clickbait detection, and queues submission for the optimal window.

**Skills:** Platform analysis, timing optimization, content formatting

## 15. Pricing Page Intelligence

**Prompt:**
> "Monitor pricing pages of my top 20 competitors. Alert me to any changes. Analyze positioning shifts."

When Competitor A drops their pro tier price by 20%, you know within hours. When they add a new feature to Enterprise, you know.

Advanced: The agent generates recommended responses to competitive pricing moves.

**Skills:** Price monitoring, competitive intelligence, strategy recommendations
