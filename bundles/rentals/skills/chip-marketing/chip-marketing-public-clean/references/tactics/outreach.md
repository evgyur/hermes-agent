# Outreach Tactics

## 6. Micro-Influencer Outreach Machine

Give an agent runtime your ICP definition. It:
- Scrapes X/Twitter/LinkedIn for micro-influencers (1K-50K followers) in your niche
- Analyzes their recent content
- Identifies genuine interest overlap with your product
- Generates personalized outreach based on specific posts
- Sends DMs or emails
- Tracks response rates and follows up
- Schedules calls with positive responses

Personalization isn't "Hey [NAME], loved your post about [TOPIC]." It's actually reading their last 20 posts and finding genuine connection points.

**Skills:** Social scraping, personalization engine, outreach automation

## 11. Cold Email Personalizer

Give an agent runtime a list of target accounts. For each:
- Finds decision-maker LinkedIn profiles
- Reads their recent posts, company announcements, job listings
- Identifies pain points from public information
- Generates hyper-personalized email referencing specific details
- Finds verified email addresses
- Sends through your email tool
- Tracks opens, clicks, replies
- Generates follow-up sequences based on engagement

This is "I noticed you're hiring for X and your last quarter report mentioned Y" level personalization at scale.

**Skills:** Contact research, personalization, email automation

## 12. Community Infiltrator

an agent runtime joins relevant Slack workspaces, Discord servers, Facebook groups.

It doesn't spam. It monitors for questions your product could answer. When someone asks "What's the best tool for X?" and X is your category, you get an alert with conversation context and a suggested response.

You show up as a helpful community member who happens to have built the exact thing they need.

**Skills:** Community monitoring, opportunity detection, response drafting

## 13. Partnership Scout

**Prompt:**
> "Find companies that serve my target audience but aren't competitors. Analyze their content for partnership signals. Generate outreach for co-marketing opportunities."

an agent runtime identifies potential partners, researches their marketing activities, and generates custom partnership pitches.

Example: "Hey, I noticed you wrote about [topic] and we have complementary data. Want to co-author a report?"

**Skills:** Partner research, opportunity matching, outreach automation

## 14. Newsletter Growth Engine

For newsletter operators:
- Monitors for cross-promotion opportunities with newsletters in adjacent niches
- Analyzes subscriber overlap potential
- Generates swap proposals
- Manages logistics

Also monitors your unsubscribes, analyzes patterns (what content causes churn?), and suggests topic adjustments.

**Skills:** Newsletter research, swap coordination, churn analysis
