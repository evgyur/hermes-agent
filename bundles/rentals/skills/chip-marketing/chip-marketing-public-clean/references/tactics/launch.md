# Launch Tactics

## 1. 100-Platform Launch Blitz

Feed an agent runtime a list of 100+ launch directories (Product Hunt, Uneed, Micro Launch, DevHunt, BetaList, Futurepedia, LaunchingNext, NextGen Tools, etc.).

**Prompt:**
> "Submit my SaaS to all of these. Use the assets in ./launch-assets/ for logos and banners. Stagger submissions over 30 days. Generate platform-specific descriptions optimized for each site's audience. Track approval status. Alert me when we go live anywhere."

**Skills:** Browser automation, file upload, scheduling, multi-site form submission

**Impact:** Continuous visibility across the launch ecosystem instead of a single Product Hunt spike. Most directories have 3-6 month waitlists for free submissions; an agent runtime monitors them all and pings you when spots open.

## 2. Backlink Hunter

Scan your niche for broken links on high-DA sites. When a dead link is found pointing to content similar to yours:
- Draft personalized outreach email
- Find site owner's contact info
- Send pitch offering your content as replacement
- Follow up after 3 days if no response
- Log everything to your CRM

**Skills:** Browser automation, email automation, web scraping

**Real result:** 47 backlinks in a month from a single prompt.
