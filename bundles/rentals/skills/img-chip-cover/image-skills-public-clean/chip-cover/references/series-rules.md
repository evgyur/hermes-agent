# Series rules

A cover series needs fixed DNA and variable storytelling.

Fixed DNA:

- canvas/aspect ratio per platform;
- logo position and safe zone;
- headline treatment;
- color system;
- CTA/badge rules;
- recurring visual motif.

Variable elements:

- metaphor/key art;
- layout direction;
- depth/lighting;
- background scene;
- badge or source marker.

Do not finalize a new cover family after one image. Make at least three different covers, QA them together, then write/update the brand pack.
