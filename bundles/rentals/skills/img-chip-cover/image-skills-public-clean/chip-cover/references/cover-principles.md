# Cover principles

A strong cover is a visual thesis, not decoration.

Rules:

- sell the conflict, outcome, or capability;
- make one idea readable in five seconds on a phone;
- keep the headline short and high-contrast;
- reserve safe zones for platform UI and cropping;
- use real brand assets, not generated fake logos;
- separate generated key art from deterministic text overlays;
- avoid dense infographics unless the task explicitly asks for a diagram;
- prefer one strong metaphor over many small objects;
- keep series DNA consistent but vary composition across topics.
