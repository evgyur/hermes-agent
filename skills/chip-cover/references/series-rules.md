# Series rules

A cover series shares recognizable DNA but varies enough to avoid template fatigue.

## Fixed DNA

Fix logo zone, CTA role, footer role, type personality, color roles, safe margins, hierarchy, contrast, platform sizes, QA checklist, and allowed composition archetypes.

## Variable parts

Vary central metaphor/object, background scene, crop/angle, composition archetype, density, badge, card type, highlight color variant, and topic-specific source artifact.

## Freeze rule

Do not freeze a new family after one cover. First render three different topics, preserve same DNA, vary composition/metaphor, QA all, then write `brands/<brand>/DESIGN.md`, examples, renderer notes, and manifest.
