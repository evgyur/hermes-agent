# Existing-participant / workshop-graduate promo pattern

Use when the audience already passed the Human20 workshop and the image promotes staying in `Среда внедрения ИИ` rather than joining/subscribing from outside.

## Positioning

- Frame Среда as the next layer after the main route: practice, chat, Pulse, meetings, materials, API/MCP.
- Avoid channel-subscription framing. The reader is not being acquired into a Telegram channel; they are choosing whether to remain in the working environment.
- Good headline shape: `Воркшоп пройден. Не теряйте среду.` or `Среда открыта — продолжаем внедрять ИИ`.
- Badge: `ДЛЯ ВЫПУСКНИКОВ ВОРКШОПА` or `HUMAN20 / СРЕДА`.

## CTA

Public/channel covers normally use `Подписаться: @human20`. For existing-participant payment promos, use an action CTA instead:

- `Остаться в Среде`
- `Продлить доступ к Среде`
- `Оплатить Среду`

Show the payment URL nearby, e.g. `human20.app/payment`.

## Card content

Useful chips:

- `1 800 ₽ / месяц`
- `до конца недели`
- `не новый курс`

Useful code/product lines:

- `workshop_access: уже пройден`
- `+ community_access / telegram_access`
- `+ живые разборы, обновления и агентский контекст`

## QA

In addition to normal Human20 cover QA, verify:

- It does not look like a generic channel subscription ad.
- The CTA is about staying/continuing, not subscribing.
- The workshop graduate audience is explicit.
- Payment/link information is legible.
