# Human20 cover visual contract

This reference captures the approved cover family from the Hermes hotfix post.

## Fixed brand elements

- Canvas: square 1080×1080 PNG.
- Background: dark navy/black gradient with indigo glow.
- Logo: official Human20 lockup in the top-left from `human20-app/frontend-v2/public/brand`.
- CTA: large bottom button with exact text `Подписаться: @human20`.
- Footer: `Человек 2.0 · Среда внедрения ИИ` and `human20.app`.

## Variable elements

- Badge: short uppercase topic label, e.g. `HERMES HOTFIX`, `OPENCLAW`, `AI WORKFLOW`.
- Headline: 1–2 lines in Russian, large and phone-readable.
- Subtitle: optional technical/product context.
- Card title: source/repo/product name.
- Chips: 2–4 short labels.
- Code/product lines: 2–4 lines with tones `error`, `success`, `neutral`, `muted`.

## QA failure examples

- invented logo instead of official Human20 lockup;
- missing CTA or wrong CTA spelling;
- typo in repo/product name;
- cropped text;
- random AI-art objects unrelated to the post;
- generic tech background without the central card.
